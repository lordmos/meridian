# Minimalist

**Monochrome ink, clean geometry.**

Stroke-only outer ring, four cardinal ticks for the meridian motif, solid-fill letter at center. Single brand color; everything else is ink or white. Works in light or dark mode without adjustment.

## Fit

- CLI tools, libraries, dev utilities
- Docs-first projects where typography and whitespace carry the identity
- Projects that want to look timeless, not trendy

## Typography

Display: **Inter** (geometric sans, neutral). Mono: **JetBrains Mono**.

## Icons

Lucide outline, single-color stroke via `currentColor`.
