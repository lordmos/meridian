# Enterprise

**Navy medallion, confident geometry.**

Filled navy circle with a subtle inner white ring, four white triangular compass points, heavy-weight white letter at center. Reads as "trustworthy, institutional, shipping-grade" without tipping into stuffy.

## Fit

- B2B platforms, infrastructure, compliance-sensitive tools
- Orgs that have customer-facing stakeholders in procurement
- Projects where "reassuring" matters as much as "cool"

## Typography

Display: **SF Pro Display / Segoe UI / Source Sans Pro** (readable geometric sans with institutional weight). Mono: **JetBrains Mono**. Slightly heavier body weights (450–500) than Minimalist's.

## Icons

Lucide **filled** variants preferred (more weight, less craft-y). Falls back to outline when filled isn't available.
